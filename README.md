Independent Open-Source Software Suite

ELPO develops independent desktop applications designed to make everyday computing more understandable, efficient and controllable.

Our software

Sysora

Understand your PC. Monitor performance, diagnose problems and explore system activity.

Atlas

Manage your workspaces. Organize and restore your working environments.

Forge

Understand your development environment and diagnose configuration problems.

Trace

Explore your computer's history and understand what has changed.

Principles

- Open source under permissive licenses.
- Local-first architecture.
- Privacy and user control.
- No mandatory accounts or cloud services.
- No unnecessary AI integration.
- Cross-platform development whenever technically possible.
